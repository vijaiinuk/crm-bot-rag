## Who can see a report

New reports and dashboards are private until you share them. Until then, only you can open them from **Reports**.

When you share a report or dashboard, you choose a permission level for each user or team:

- **Can view**: The person can open the report and see its data, but can't change it.
- **Can edit**: The person can open the report, change it in **Report builder**, and update its sharing settings.

Sharing a report doesn't override what people can access elsewhere in the product. A report only shows data from modules the person viewing it can use. If you share a report that draws on a module someone isn't licensed for, that person sees only the data from modules they can use.

Report data refreshes every 15 minutes, so people you share with may see slightly older figures than you see on screen right after a change.

## Share with a team

1. Go to **Reports → Dashboards**, or open a saved report from **Reports**.
2. Open the report or dashboard you want to share, then click **Share**.
3. In the sharing box, type the name of a team or user, then select it from the list. Choose **Can view** or **Can edit** for that team or user. Repeat to add more.
4. Click **Save**.

The team can now find the report or dashboard in **Reports**. To change access later, open **Share** again, pick a different permission, or remove the team or user, then click **Save**.

If you want a team to receive a report by email instead, you can set up a daily, weekly or monthly CSV or XLSX export in **Scheduled exports**. Each export can go to up to 10 recipients.
