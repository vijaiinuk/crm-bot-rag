## Choose a template

A template controls the layout of the quote PDF that your customer receives by email. The template you choose applies to that quote only, so you can use different templates for different customers.

1. Go to **Quotes → Quote builder** and open an existing **Draft** quote, or create a new quote from a deal.
2. In the **Template** field, open the list and select a template. The preview on the right updates to show the layout.
3. Review the preview, then select **Save**. The quote keeps its number (for example, Q-00001) and its **Draft** status.

If you change the template after a quote is **Sent**, the customer's copy doesn't change. Create a new quote if you need to send a different layout.

## Customise branding

Branding settings change how the template looks, such as the logo, colours and footer text. Your changes apply to the template you selected for the quote.

1. Go to **Quotes → Quote builder** and open the quote you want to change.
2. Select the **Branding** tab.
3. Next to **Logo**, select **Upload** and choose a PNG or JPG file. The file can be up to 2 MB. A logo at least 400 pixels wide gives the sharpest result in the PDF.
4. Set **Accent colour** to match your brand. The accent colour is used for headings and the line item header row. Then edit **Footer text** to add details such as your company address or payment terms.
5. Check the preview, then select **Save**. When you're ready to send the quote, select **Send quote**. The PDF attachment uses your branding.

If the quote needs approval because of a discount above 20%, the branding stays with the quote while it is **Pending approval**. You can send it once the status is **Approved**.
