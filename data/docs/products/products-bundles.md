## Create a bundle

1. Go to **Products → Bundles**.
2. Select **New bundle**, then enter a name and a unique SKU for the bundle.
3. Select **Add products**, search by name or SKU, and tick each product you want to include. A bundle must contain at least 2 products and no more than 50. Archived products don't appear in the search results, so unarchive a product first if you need it in the bundle.
4. Under **Pricing**, choose how the bundle is priced: **Sum of products** or **Fixed bundle price**. If you choose a fixed price, enter the amount for each currency you sell in.
5. Select **Save**.

The bundle now appears in the **Bundles** list, and you can add it to new quotes like any other item.

## Bundle pricing

A bundle's price is calculated in one of two ways. You choose the method when you create the bundle and can change it later by editing the bundle.

With **Sum of products**, the bundle price is the total of the prices of the products it contains. The prices come from the price book in use. If you change a product's price in a price book, the bundle price changes with it. This option suits bundles that should always reflect current catalogue prices.

With **Fixed bundle price**, the bundle sells at the amount you enter, no matter what the individual products cost. Use this option when you want to offer a set package price, such as a starter kit that is cheaper than buying each item separately. Changes to the prices of the individual products don't affect the bundle price.

Your account can have up to 20 price books, and exactly one is the default. Each price book holds one price per currency. Keep this in mind when you use **Sum of products**, because the total depends on which price book applies and on whether every product in the bundle has a price in the currency you need.

If you archive a product that is part of a bundle, the product stays on quotes that already include it, but you can't add it to new quotes.
