## Schedule a meeting

1. Go to **Activities → Calendar**.
2. Select **New meeting**, or open the record you want to link the meeting to and select **New meeting** in the activity panel.
3. Enter a **Title**, then choose the **Date**, **Start time** and **Duration**. The default duration is 30 minutes.
4. In **Attendees**, add the people who should join. You can add teammates and contacts from the linked record. Add a **Location** or a video link if you have one.
5. Select **Save**. The meeting appears on your **Calendar** and in the activity panel of the linked record.

To change a meeting later, select it on the **Calendar**, edit the fields and select **Save**.

## Calendar sync

**Calendar sync** connects your Google or Microsoft 365 calendar to your **Calendar** in the CRM. Changes flow both ways and are picked up every 5 minutes. A meeting you schedule in the CRM appears in your external calendar, and an event you create there appears in the CRM.

1. Go to **Activities → Calendar**.
2. Select **Calendar sync**.
3. Choose **Google** or **Microsoft 365**.
4. Sign in to your calendar account and approve the access request.
5. Back in the CRM, choose which of your calendars to sync. Select **Save**.
6. Wait up to 5 minutes, then check that events from your external calendar appear on the **Calendar**.

If an event doesn't appear after 5 minutes, reopen **Calendar sync** and confirm the correct calendar is selected. Each user connects their own calendar, so syncing your calendar doesn't change anyone else's.

## Meeting notes

Meeting notes are stored on the meeting itself, so anyone who opens it can read them. Open a meeting from the **Calendar** or from the activity panel of the linked record, and write in the **Notes** field. You can add notes before the meeting, such as an agenda or questions, and update them during or afterward with decisions and next steps. Notes save when you select **Save**.

Because a meeting is linked to a record, its notes also appear in that record's activity history. Colleagues who work on the same record can see what was discussed without asking you.

To follow up on something from a meeting, create a task from the meeting's notes. Give it a single assignee and a due date, and optionally set a reminder from 5 minutes to 7 days before it's due. The task then shows in **My tasks**, and it turns red there if it becomes overdue.

If the conversation happened by phone instead of in a meeting, use **Log a call** in the activity panel of the record and pick an outcome: **Connected**, **Left voicemail**, **No answer** or **Wrong number**.
