## Log a call

Log a call from the record you spoke to or about, such as a contact, company or deal. The call log appears in the record's activity history.

1. Open the record the call relates to.
2. In the activity panel, select **Log a call**.
3. Enter the details of the call: the date and time, the duration, and your notes on what was discussed. The date and time default to now, so change them if you are logging a call that happened earlier.
4. Select an outcome, then select **Save**.

The call log appears in the record's activity history, and you can see your own call logs alongside the rest of the record's activity. If you need to follow up, create a task on the same record so it appears in **Activities → My tasks**. A task has one assignee, a due date and an optional reminder.

## Call outcomes

Every call log has one outcome. Choose the one that best describes how the call ended.

| Outcome | When to use it |
|---|---|
| **Connected** | You spoke with the person. Use this even if the conversation was short. |
| **Left voicemail** | The call went to voicemail and you left a message. |
| **No answer** | Nobody picked up and you did not leave a message. |
| **Wrong number** | The number did not reach the person or company you intended. |

If you reach voicemail but do not leave a message, choose **No answer**. Choose **Wrong number** when the number itself is the problem, so that colleagues know the contact details on the record may need checking.

## Edit a call log

You can change a call log after you save it, for example to correct the outcome or add notes you wrote down later.

1. Open the record the call was logged against and find the call log in the activity history.
2. Open the call log and change the outcome, date and time, duration or notes as needed.
3. Select **Save**.

The call log shows your updated details as soon as you save.
