## Email notifications

Choose which events send you an email. Changes apply as soon as you save them.

1. Click your profile picture in the top-right corner and select **My settings**.
2. Open the **Notifications** tab, then select **Email**.
3. Turn each event on or off. For example, you can turn on **Assigned to you** and **Mentioned in a note**, and turn off **Task due today**. Events that are on show a blue switch.
4. Click **Save changes**. A confirmation message appears at the top of the page.

If you stop receiving an email you expect, check this tab first. Also check that the event is still switched on.

## In-app notifications

In-app notifications appear under the bell icon (**Notifications**) at the top of every page. The app keeps them for 90 days, then removes them automatically.

1. Go to **My settings → Notifications** and select **In-app**.
2. Turn each event on or off. The list matches the email list, so you can receive an event in the app, by email, both, or neither.
3. Click **Save changes**. New notifications show a count on the bell icon, and you can click the bell at any time to read them.

To clear the count without opening each item, open the bell and select **Mark all as read**. The notifications stay in the list until the 90 days have passed.

## Quiet hours

Use quiet hours to pause notifications at set times, such as evenings or weekends. Your event choices stay the same, and quiet hours only control when notifications reach you.

1. Go to **My settings → Notifications** and select **Quiet hours**.
2. Turn on **Pause notifications during quiet hours**, then set the **From** and **To** times and select the days they apply to. Quiet hours use the time zone in your profile.
3. Click **Save changes**.

During quiet hours the app doesn't email you or show a count on the bell. Events that happen in that period still appear under **Notifications** when you next open it. To pause notifications immediately, set the **From** time to a few minutes from now. To resume, turn off **Pause notifications during quiet hours** and save.
