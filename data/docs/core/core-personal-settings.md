## Profile and photo

Your profile shows your name, job title, and photo to colleagues across the CRM. Update it whenever your details change.

1. Click your profile picture in the top-right corner, then select **My settings**.
2. On the **Profile** tab, edit your **Name**, **Job title**, and **Phone** fields. To change your photo, click **Upload photo** and choose a JPG or PNG file from your computer.
3. Click **Save changes**. Your new details appear next to your name the next time a colleague opens your record or sees you mentioned.

Your photo is also what appears in the top-right corner of every page, so you can check there that the new image uploaded correctly.

## Language and time zone

Language controls the labels, menus, and messages you see. Time zone controls how dates and times are shown to you, including the timestamps on notifications under the bell icon (**Notifications**). Your choices affect only your own view, not your colleagues'.

1. Click your profile picture in the top-right corner, then select **My settings**.
2. On the **Regional** tab, choose your preferred option in the **Language** list and in the **Time zone** list.
3. Click **Save changes**. Reload the page if the menus still show the previous language.

If times on records look wrong, check the **Time zone** field first. It is the most common cause of meetings and reminders appearing an hour or more off.

## Email signature

Your email signature is added to the end of messages you send from the CRM. You can include your name, role, phone number, and a short line such as your company website.

1. Click your profile picture in the top-right corner, then select **My settings**.
2. Open the **Email signature** tab.
3. Type your signature in the editor. Use the toolbar to apply bold or italics, add a link, or insert a small image such as a logo.
4. Click **Save changes**, then click **Send test email** to receive a sample at your own address and confirm the signature looks the way you expect.

Keep the signature short, as long blocks of text and large images can push the content of your message out of view. To remove your signature later, return to the **Email signature** tab, clear the editor, and save.
